Task 4 – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Report

About the data The dataset has sales orders for 2025. After cleaning, it has 971 orders, total revenue of ₹27,641,715 and 2,911 units sold. The average order value is ₹28,467.

Data cleaning summary

  • Removed 15 duplicate rows.
  • Fixed spacing and spelling in product, region and payment mode.
  • Filled 40 missing regions with "Unknown" and 21 missing discounts with 0.
  • Removed 25 rows with missing quantity and 4 rows with extreme quantities (60 to 120), which looked like entry mistakes.

Key findings

  1. Electronics drives the business.Electronics gives about 86% of revenue, Furniture about 14%, and Stationery less than 1%.
  2. Laptop is the top product (₹7.27 million), followed by Smartphone and Monitor.
  3. South is the best region (₹7.91 million) and North is the lowest of the named regions (₹5.02 million). About ₹1.32 million of revenue has no region ("Unknown").
  4. December is the best month (₹3.36 million) and May is the weakest (₹1.29 million). October is also high, which may be because of festive-season buying.
  5. Revenue has a strong correlation with unit price (0.85), a weaker one with quantity (0.37), and almost none with discount (-0.08).
  6. There are 58 high-value orders (above about ₹92,000), all for Laptops or Smartphones. They are only about 6% of orders but make up about a third of revenue, so they are real big orders and not errors.
  7. Consumers bring most of the revenue (54%), then Corporate (33%) and Small Business (13%). Debit card is the most used payment mode.

Recommendation The business depends heavily on a few expensive electronics products. Keeping stock of Laptops and Smartphones, planning offers for the slow months (April to August), and promoting Furniture and Stationery could make revenue steadier.

Limitations

  • This is sample data, so the findings are for practice.
  • The "Unknown" region reduces the accuracy of region analysis.
